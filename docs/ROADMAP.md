# Public roadmap

ZeroRun v0.4 has completed its internal pytest and SymPy technical qualification. The next objective is external proof, not a higher internal benchmark number.

1. Onboard 2–5 real developers or teams on their own repositories.
2. Collect at least 100 genuine chronological agent or CI edit → test requests inside the supported boundary.
3. Preserve broad/shared edits, failures, misses, bypasses, and verification events rather than selecting only favorable cases.
4. Publish sanitized external measurements for reuse rate, p50/p95, cumulative compute/time savings, verification outcomes, installation friction, and support burden.
5. Validate buyer ownership, willingness to pay, retention, and paid-pilot economics.
6. Expand the supported product boundary only when new workloads earn their own safety and qualification evidence.

The internal 5× compute-efficiency target is no longer an open v0.4 gate: the accepted pytest and SymPy receipts both cleared it. That does not imply every customer workload will achieve 5× or that total CI spend will fall by the same amount.

Roadmap items are intentions, not commitments or completed milestones.
